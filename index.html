About us

A small clinic that takes its time

BrightPath Dental opened on South Lamar in 2012. We started it because we kept hearing the same two complaints about dentists: it hurt more than it should, and nobody knew what anything cost until the bill arrived.

So we run things the other way round. Appointments are not rushed, numbing is done properly, and every treatment comes with the price in writing before we begin. If something can wait, we will tell you it can wait.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Emily Chen, DDS

Dr. Chen has been a dentist for 14 years, the last nine of them here at BrightPath. Patients tend to remember two things about her: she explains what she is about to do before she does it, and she checks — more than once — that you are properly numb.

She looks after most of the clinic's clear aligner cases and is usually the first to say when a tooth does not need treatment at all. She is supported by our two hygienists, Maria and Jess, who handle most cleanings.
